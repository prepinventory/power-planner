What this means for you

    Before you plug in

    • Cover the panels first. A panel has no off switch; any light makes it live. Cover them with a blanket or tarp, or lay them face-down, before you connect or disconnect anything. DC sparks don't snuff themselves out the way wall power does.
    • Measure before you connect. Uncover the panels and check voltage and polarity (plus on plus) at the plug end with a multimeter. With nothing plugged in, no current flows. Then cover, plug in, and uncover.
    • Match your connectors. MC4 plugs from different brands can fit loosely, heat up and melt. The electrical code requires connectors that plug together to be the same brand and type, unless both makers list them as compatible.
    • Parallel takes a Y-branch splitter. Three or more panels in parallel usually need an in-line fuse on each panel.
    • Tie the panels down and keep the connectors off wet ground.
