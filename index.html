A one-day snapshot: what your gear can run, for how long, and what to watch for.

Numbers are estimates — check your own labels and manuals, or better yet, measure with a plug-in watt meter. Never backfeed a house through an outlet: hard-wired devices like well pumps and whole-house generator hookups need a transfer switch or interlock kit installed by an electrician. Run generators outdoors only, far from doors and windows, and keep a battery CO alarm inside. From The Blackout Brief.
